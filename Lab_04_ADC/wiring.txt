Lab 04: ADC Read Wiring | 接线

STM32F103C8T6 (Blue Pill):
  PA0 (ADC1_CH0)  →  Potentiometer middle pin (wiper)
  3.3V            →  Potentiometer left pin
  GND             →  Potentiometer right pin

Optional: PA2 (TX) → USB-TTL RX for serial output

Notes:
  - ADC range: 0-4095 (12-bit) = 0-3.3V
  - Turn potentiometer to vary voltage 0-3.3V
